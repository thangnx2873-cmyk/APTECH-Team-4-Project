Vinschool Launches New International Curriculum for 2025

Vinschool Admin

Curriculum

28

Vinschool proudly announces the launch of its enhanced international curriculum for the 2025-2026 academic year. This innovative program integrates global standards with Vietnamese values, focusing on critical thinking, creativity, and digital literacy to prepare students for a rapidly evolving world.

The new curriculum includes advanced STEM modules, AI and coding from Grade 1, and expanded bilingual education across all levels. Developed in collaboration with leading international education experts, it ensures Vinschool students receive a world-class education while preserving cultural identity.

Key Features of the New Curriculum

The curriculum emphasizes project-based learning, where students apply knowledge to real-world challenges. From early childhood to high school, learners engage in interdisciplinary projects that develop problem-solving skills and teamwork. Technology integration includes smart classrooms, VR learning experiences, and personalized learning platforms.

Global Partnerships and Teacher Development

To support this ambitious curriculum, Vinschool has partnered with Cambridge Assessment International Education and Microsoft Education. Over 300 teachers completed specialized training in inquiry-based teaching and digital pedagogy. The program also includes ongoing professional development to maintain teaching excellence.
