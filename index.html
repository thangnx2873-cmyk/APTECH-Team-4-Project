Welcome to Vinschool Education System

As Vietnam's largest K-12 education system and the first fully accredited by the Council of International Schools (CIS), Vinschool nurtures global citizens with a strong Vietnamese identity, critical thinking, and lifelong learning skills.

Best Education for Global Citizens

Founded in 2013, Vinschool is Vietnam's largest K-12 system with 54 campuses serving nearly 48,000 students. We integrate Cambridge international curriculum with Vietnamese values to develop critical thinking, leadership, and cultural pride.

  • CIS Accredited & Cambridge Certified
  • Expert Teachers Trained Internationally
